Apartment price per m² in capital cities, 10 years

A monthly pipeline joins BIS residential property price indices with ECB exchange rates and city price anchors to model price per m² for 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom apartments across 17 capitals. Pick up to two cities and the apartment sizes to compare.
